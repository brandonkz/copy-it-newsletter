Who runs this

Copy It is a free weekly newsletter from Brandon, a creator in South Africa. For the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), Brandon is the responsible party.

What we collect

  • The email address you type into a signup form.
  • Which form you used: the one at the top of the home page (hero) or the one at the bottom (footer).
  • Campaign details that were already in the link you opened, if any: utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_term, and utm_content. The page stores those in this browser tab (session storage) so they are still there when you submit the form.
  • The address of the Copy It page you signed up on, and a label that the signup is for Copy It. The email list is shared with another newsletter, and these details keep Copy It readers separate.
